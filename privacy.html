Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data EndlessDevs ("we", "us") collects when you play Germline, why, and what you can do about it. We are the data controller. Contact: support@endlessdevs.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Steam account ID – when you use online features (ranked play, leaderboards, PvP, co-op, friend challenges, Showcase sharing, the Helix shop). Steam handles sign-in; we never see your Steam password.
  • Online game data – progress, rankings, match results, published Showcase content, and records of in-game purchases needed to deliver what you bought.
  • Steam friend information – to check that friend-only features (for example sharing with a friend) go to a real Steam friend.
  • Crash reports – if the game crashes: the game version, error details and a random install identifier. Crash reports are not linked to your Steam account.
  • Feedback – what you write when you send feedback or a bug report, and an email address only if you choose to give one for a reply.
  • IP address – used briefly in memory to limit abuse (rate limiting). We do not store it.

Offline play does not send personal data to us.

Why we use it (lawful basis)

  • To provide the online features you ask for, and deliver purchases – performance of a contract.
  • To fix crashes, prevent cheating and abuse, and keep the service secure – legitimate interests.
  • To reply to feedback when you give an email – consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

How long we keep it

  • Online account data: until you delete your online account, or we close the online service.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Feedback: as long as needed to deal with it. Email us to have it removed sooner.

Who we share it with

  • Valve (Steam) – sign-in, friends, and payments. Steam's own privacy policy applies to that data.
  • Our hosting provider – stores data on our behalf under a data processing agreement.
  • Other players – your rankings and anything you publish to Showcase are visible to other players.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else unless the law requires it. If data is stored outside the UK, we use appropriate safeguards such as adequacy regulations or standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your data, or object to or restrict how we use it. To delete your online account and its data, email us and include your Steam profile link so we can find your account. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

The online features are not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the game or on Steam.
