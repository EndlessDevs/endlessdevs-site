About Germline

Germline is an idle growth game about living things. Raise one of 60 hand-drawn species, from tigers and bees to corals, octopuses and peacocks, through four life stages: Founder, Developed, Juvenile and Adult. Your first adult of each species unlocks two more.

Every organism is born with a seed, and a few percent of births are rare colour morphs such as albino, golden and ghost. Reproduce to roll a new seed, crossbreed two species for better odds and record every find in the Bestiary. Farmers keep your economy running, fighters are built for battle, and underneath it all you build a working body of connected organs, automate it, and expand into networks and new universes.

  • 60 hand-drawn species, each with art for all four life stages
  • Rare colour morphs and crossbreeding
  • Farmers and fighters with their own stats
  • Active and idle progression with offline growth
  • Resets that carry permanent upgrades, challenges and ecosystems
  • Photo mode
